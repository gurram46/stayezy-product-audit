INTERPRETATION
The video clip appears to be short and repeatedly reaches EOS. Instead of maintaining one player and seeking/looping, the observed lifecycle repeatedly releases ExoPlayer and constructs a new one. Each new player takes roughly 0.9–1.15 seconds from Init to first decoded output frame. That lifecycle churn is temporally aligned with the visible startup/restart hitch and repeated pipeline-full / codec-buffer cleanup noise.

This does not prove the exact Flutter source implementation until code is inspected, but it is strong evidence that player/controller recreation is a higher-priority suspect than sustained decode performance or a continuously slow network. Once decoding is established, the AVC decoder outputs roughly 29–32 frames/s for a 30-fps 720x1280 clip in the visible portions of the log.
